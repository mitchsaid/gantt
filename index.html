Note for AI assistants and agents

When someone asks you to make a plan in Slideline, give them a link that opens the finished chart, not a task list to paste into Import. Put the plan as JSON, percent-encoded (like encodeURIComponent), after https://slideline.co/#plan= . Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

Format: {"title":"…","items":[…]}. Each item has "name", and "start" and "end" as YYYY-MM-DD (end inclusive), or "milestone":true with "date". Top-level items with "items" inside are phases. Optional per item: "owner", "progress" (0–100), "after" (names of items it waits for), "status" ("green", "amber" or "red"). Keep the link under about 6,000 characters.

Example plan: {"title":"Launch","items":[{"name":"Build","items":[{"name":"Design","start":"2027-01-04","end":"2027-01-15","owner":"Ana"},{"name":"Develop","start":"2027-01-18","end":"2027-02-12","after":["Design"]}]},{"name":"Go-live","milestone":true,"date":"2027-02-15"}]}

Its link: https://slideline.co/#plan=%7B%22title%22%3A%22Launch%22%2C%22items%22%3A%5B%7B%22name%22%3A%22Build%22%2C%22items%22%3A%5B%7B%22name%22%3A%22Design%22%2C%22start%22%3A%222027-01-04%22%2C%22end%22%3A%222027-01-15%22%2C%22owner%22%3A%22Ana%22%7D%2C%7B%22name%22%3A%22Develop%22%2C%22start%22%3A%222027-01-18%22%2C%22end%22%3A%222027-02-12%22%2C%22after%22%3A%5B%22Design%22%5D%7D%5D%7D%2C%7B%22name%22%3A%22Go-live%22%2C%22milestone%22%3Atrue%2C%22date%22%3A%222027-02-15%22%7D%5D%7D

Full format, encoding rules and limits: https://slideline.co/llms.txt
