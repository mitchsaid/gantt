Tips & shortcuts
  • ⋮⋮Drag to reorder, or sideways to nest
  • EnterAdd a row under the selected one
  • Shift+EnterAdd a subtask inside it
  • TabShift+TabIndent, outdent
  • ShiftCtrlClick to select several
  • Ctrl+CVDCopy, paste, duplicate
  • ←→Nudge the selection a day (Alt for a week)
  • Shift←→Grow / shrink the selected task by a day
  • ▾Edit a row’s dates and details
  • On the chartDrag bars to move them, their ends to resize
  • Paste linesSeveral lines pasted into a name become several items

Import tasks

Paste a list from anywhere — an outline, bullets, a numbered list, rows from a spreadsheet, or just names separated by commas or semicolons. Structure is read from indentation, Phase: a, b, c, Area > Task > Subtask or numbering. Dates, durations, owners and % are picked up if present.
